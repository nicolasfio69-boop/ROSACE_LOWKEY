Junior web developer · Kaarna Health (fictional)
Job description

Company: Kaarna Health is a fictional start-up building an appointment app for physiotherapy clinics in Espoo and Vantaa.
Location: Leppävaara, Espoo. Fully on-site for the first three months, then hybrid.
Team: four developers and one designer; releases every two weeks.
Working language: English in the team; the support person talks with clinics in Finnish.
What you will do:
- Build screens in React and TypeScript from the designer's mock-ups.
- Write automated tests for the booking flow. Nothing ships until the tests pass and a colleague has reviewed the code.
- Follow the health-data rules: no patient data in logs or test fixtures.
- Plan your own tickets for the two-week sprint, and say early when one will slip.
- Work every day with the designer and the other developers.
What success looks like: you ship a small feature end to end, with tests, in your second sprint.
Support: pair programming twice a week, a design system, and a clear definition of done.
